Une Faible Impression — Set Rigging
Wind gust rig — paper lift, park bench
Sheet: 10 × 10 in standard paper
Effect: lift → up + sideways → exit high frame corner
1 — Side view (camera's line of sight)
2 — Top view (camera in front, wind from left)
Wind source
Electric fan or blower, fully out of frame. Variable speed + on-cue trigger (remote or operator switch). Quiet as practical — production sound.
Paper-lift airflow
Broad, diffuse gust — enough to lift a 10 × 10 in sheet from a seated lap and carry it up toward the high frame corner. Repeatable take to take.
Power
Confirm draw, outlet type, and cable length before load-in. Tape and mat all runs; keep clear of camera and crew movement.
Outdoors
Sandbag stands and fan. Test against natural wind. Watch hair, clothing, and leaves in the gust path. Keep footprint small for a park location.
General wind (dual mode)
Same fan/rig also runs continuously as a general wind source for medium-to-wide shots — strong enough to visibly move hair, loose clothing, and leaves in frame, not just lift the paper. Re-aim from the upward, bench-only burst to a level sweep across the full shot; confirm sustained-run speed setting separately from the cued-burst setting.
