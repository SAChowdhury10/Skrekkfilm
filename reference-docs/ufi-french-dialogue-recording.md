# French Dialogue Recording Script

Purpose: Read each line aloud in French and record it. The actors will use your recordings as a pronunciation reference for their own performance, so natural pace and clear diction matter more than dramatic acting.

How to use this doc: Lines are numbered in script order. Please save each recording as `Line-01.mp3`, `Line-02.mp3`, etc. (or similar), matching the numbers below. English translations are included only so you understand the meaning/emotion — you don't need to read them aloud.

---

## Part 1 — Full French Lines

**Line 1** — MAN (V.O.) — Park bench, writing to his mother (1st attempt, then scratched out)
> Chère Maman... Je pense à toi.

*(Dear Mom... I miss you.)*

---

**Line 2** — MAN (V.O.) — 2nd attempt at the letter
> Chère Maman... Je réfléchissais à l'idée de marche en avant... Et ça m'a rappelé notre voyage en train de Toulouse à... Je ne sais plus où...

*(Dear Mom... I have started thinking about progress... And it reminded me of our train ride from Toulouse to... I can't remember where...)*

---

**Line 3** — MAN (V.O.) — 3rd/final attempt at the letter
> Maman... Je repensais à notre dernière conversation... Que voulais-tu dire quand tu as dit : « Il faut entendre le mot avec la résonance du monde. » Maman, dis-moi... parce que le mot--

*(Mom... I was thinking about our last conversation... What did you mean when you said, "One must hear the word with the sound of the world?" Mother, tell me... because the word--)*

*(Note: this line cuts off mid-sentence — trail off naturally, don't land hard on the final word.)*

---

**Line 4** — WOMAN (O.S.) — an unseen voice answering Man's search for a word
> Appelle...

*(Beckons/calls — this is the word Man is trying to remember.)*

*(Note: this exact line repeats 3 separate times in the script (see Lines 4a/4b/4c below). Please record it 3 times with the same reading each time, OR record once and we'll reuse the file — your call.)*

**Line 4a** — WOMAN (O.S.) — repeat
> Appelle...

**Line 4b** — WOMAN (O.S.) — repeat
> Appelle...

---

**Line 5** — MAN (V.O.) — writing again, now with the word recovered
> Le mot m'appelle loin du confort...

*(The word beckons me away from comfort...)*

---

**Line 6** — WOMAN — much later in the script, misspeaking a phrase (see note)
> L'avenir nous prend les bras... Or something.

*(She means to say "L'avenir nous tend les bras" — "The future awaits us" — but misspeaks it as "prend les bras," which lands closer to "The future seizes/grabs us by the arms." This is intentional...she gets the phrase slightly wrong. Please read it as written, not the "corrected" version.)*

*(Note: only "L'avenir nous prend les bras..." should be read in French — "Or something" is spoken in English by the actress and is not part of your recording.)*

## Part 2 — Mixed French/English Lines (for reference only)

These lines are spoken mostly in English but contain a French word. You don't need to record these unless we ask — just context in case it's useful:

- MAN: *"Le mot... beckons..."* (repeated 3x) — "Le mot" ("The word") is French; "beckons" is spoken in English because the character hasn't yet remembered the French word "Appelle."
