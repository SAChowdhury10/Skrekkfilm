SCENE 1EXT. PARK BENCH — SUNRISE
1A · Letter, three tries
MAN sits on a bench, sun rising behind him, ignored. Picks up NOTEBOOK and PEN.
MAN (V.O.)
Chère Maman... Je pense à toi. (Dear Mom... I miss you.)
Unsatisfied, scratches it out. Exhales, starts again.
MAN (V.O.)
Chère Maman... Je réfléchissais à l'idée de marche en avant... (...thinking about progress... reminded me of our train ride from Toulouse to... I can't remember where...)
Still unsatisfied, scratches out again. Final breath.
MAN (V.O.)
Maman... Que voulais-tu dire quand tu as dit: «Il faut entendre le mot avec la résonance du monde.» ...parce que le mot— (...What did you mean: "hear the word with the sound of the world"... because the word—)

1B · The lost word / WOMAN appears
MAN forgets the French word for "beckon."
WOMAN (O.S.)
Appelle... [repeats 3×: MAN doesn't notice, then searches for the source, then goes back to his notebook]
MAN remembers: Appelle. Begins writing. A WOMAN, early 30s, is seated on a parallel bench.
MAN (V.O.)
Le mot m'appelle loin du confort... (The word beckons me away from comfort...)

1E · Cigarette / wind / paper flies
MAN closes NOTEBOOK, stands, lights a CIGARETTE. WOMAN folds the map, returns to her book.
WOMAN (while reading)
If it's not translatable, why do we bother?
MAN (defeated)
Hmm... Feeble meaning is better than nothing at all.
A gust of wind through the trees. Both surprised.
WOMAN
Is it a poem?
MAN
Something like that. [Laughs] It doesn't rhyme.
WOMAN stands, looks into the distance. Stronger gust — MAN's SHEET OF PAPER flies away, twisting in the air. MAN chases it.
WOMAN
Hah... He has a very rebellious turn of mind...
WOMAN slowly follows. MAN picks up the fallen PAPER.

SCENE 7EXT. LOVER'S LEAP/PARK — MOMENTS LATER
7A · Translation / what is lost
MAN and WOMAN sit on a ledge. WOMAN reads from her book, pauses.
WOMAN
So you hate translations?
MAN
I don't hate them. In fact, I succumb to them.
WOMAN
But nothing can ever be transmitted in its entirety. Always a filtered remainder.
MAN
So, what is lost?
WOMAN
It's lost on me now. But, we can't help ourselves.
MAN
And why not?
#SIZEANGLE/MOVELENSNOTES
7B · Preservation / twisted words / language as filter
WOMAN contemplates.
MAN (mockingly)
Ah, so for preservation?
WOMAN
You twist my words.
MAN
You twist your own words! Language itself is a filtering of its own.
WOMAN
Language is just a mode of communication.
MAN
Yes, but not poetry. [Pause] Communication transmits what is already known. But what about the unknown? [Pause] What about the moment when something within the human being becomes too complete, too charged, too coherent to remain unexpressed... and therefore must take shape in the world?
#SIZEANGLE/MOVELENSNOTES
7C · "A becoming?"
WOMAN (mockingly)

MAN
