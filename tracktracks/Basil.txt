On the Beauty of Indie Filmmaking

It feels like people actually working together instead of a huge production. I've been on bigger sets where everyone just stands around waiting on someone else's decision. Here everyone's doing something.


What is the Meaning of Great Art

For me it's simple: does it stick with you after. Most stuff is fine in the moment and you forget it by the car ride home. The good stuff is the stuff you can't shake.


On UFI (the film)

I don't have a big monologue, my part is more about reacting and listening. That's actually harder than it sounds, because you can't really fake that on camera. People can tell.


On Tyler and Holly (our actors)

You learn a lot just from watching them work. They don't do much, but what they do is exact. It's made me pay more attention to what I'm doing when I'm not talking.


On Why We Need Your Help

Our budget is not huge for a movie, but for us it's everything, it covers food, permits, lights. If people can chip in, it actually makes a difference for something this size.
