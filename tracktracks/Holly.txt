On the Beauty of Indie Filmmaking

There is a version of every film that only exists before all the notes come in. That version is usually the truest one. Indie work protects that version.


What is the Meaning of Great Art

When I think about the best works of art, there is a sense that the artist had to make it that way. They weren't thinking about whether people would like it, whether it would be popular, or whether it would fit into what was expected of them. They were just following the work and being faithful to what it needed to become. I think that's what makes great art feel so honest. You can feel that nothing was added just to please people and nothing was taken away out of fear.


On UFI (the film)

When they first described this film to me I understood about sixty percent of it and said yes anyway, which is more or less my whole strategy for most of my good decisions. I've since understood a lot more of it, and I like it more, not less, which doesn't always happen. It's a film that trusts you to sit with something instead of explaining it to you twice. I respect that. I also occasionally miss things being explained to me twice. Both of those are true at once.


On Why We Need Your Help

There is a reason the ask is not larger. We do not want to make a bigger version of this film. We want to make this version, at this size, with these people. What the pledge does is protect the smallness. It lets us stay careful. It lets us not cut corners on the parts that would show. If you have room to give, I would love your name on the list of people who made this possible. If you do not, I understand entirely. Money is heavy right now for a lot of people. The best thing you can do in that case is watch the film when it comes out and tell someone about it.

I think of a Kickstarter pledge as a form of witness. You are saying, in advance, that you want a thing to exist. That is not a small gesture. Films need witnesses long before they have audiences. We are looking for those early witnesses. The ones who will vouch for something they have not yet seen. If that sounds like you, we would be grateful for whatever you can spare. Even the smallest tier moves us closer. And if you cannot give money, share the link. Tell one person about the film. That is also witness. That is also enough.
