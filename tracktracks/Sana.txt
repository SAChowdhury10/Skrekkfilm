
What is the Meaning of Great Art

Great art is the thing you still remember years later, long after you've forgotten what year it was. It detaches from its own timeline and just becomes part of how you think. I've forgotten entire relationships and kept a single line from a film I saw once, at the wrong age, alone. That says something about what actually lasts. It isn't the polish, it's whatever snuck past your defenses while you weren't being careful. I want to make the kind of thing that sneaks past someone the way it once snuck past me.

I don't have a grand theory of great art. I know it when something I watched at midnight is still bothering me at breakfast. That's about the extent of my critical framework, and it has never once failed me. Most things don't survive breakfast. The ones that do are usually the ones people were too scared to make in the first place. I'd rather chase that feeling than any theory about it. Theories are for people who've stopped letting themselves be surprised.
