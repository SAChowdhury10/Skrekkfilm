# UFI Kickstarter Video — Edit Script (v2)
Target runtime: ~2:15–2:20 (trim path to 2:00 included at the bottom)
10 voices, 19 cuts, all speakers featured at least once.

Built as an invitation, not a sales pitch: hook → vibe → stakes → the film → the cast → the ask.
Persuasion principles applied are noted in brackets after each beat.

---

## COLD OPEN (0:00–0:04)
**TYLER:**
> "Have I embarrassed you?"

*(Direct to camera, hold on it. No context, no title card yet — let it sit for a beat before cutting.
Source: Tyler.txt, "What is the Meaning of Great Art." This is a pattern-interrupt hook — first 30 seconds
are the highest-dropoff window of a campaign video, so open on the least expected line, not the most explanatory one.)*

Suggested title card immediately after, 1–2 sec: **UFI** — or your logo card — just enough to ground the viewer before Beat 1 starts.

---

## BEAT 1 — THE FEELING (~0:04–0:24)
Fast cuts, energetic, establishes the scrappy on-set vibe before anyone is named.

1. **SALMAN** — "You always have that feeling — the world is in front of you."
2. **BASIL** — "It feels like people actually working together instead of a huge production. Here, everyone's doing something."
3. **HOLLY** — "There's a version of every film that only exists before all the notes come in. Indie work protects that version."

*[Unity — many different people independently describing the same feeling of ownership signals this is a real, shared thing, not one person's pitch.]*

---

## BEAT 2 — WHY IT MATTERS (~0:24–0:38)
The two people who only have one section recorded — use it here, don't bury them.

4. **SANA** — "Great art is the thing you still remember years later, long after you've forgotten what year it was."
5. **SAMIHA** — "The mistake is thinking the idea is the art. Everything true happens in the doing."

*[Sets the stakes of what they're actually asking you to help make — benefit, not mechanism, per crowdfunding-video guidance: don't explain the film, show what it's reaching for.]*

## BEAT 3 — THE FILM (~0:38–0:50)
The specific hook for UFI itself.

6. **ISMAM** — "I signed on because of one page — page eleven, where nothing happens and everything happens."
7. **TYLER** — "This film feels like it's trying to hold onto something before it's gone."

## BEAT 4 — THE CAST (~0:50–1:07)
Crew talking about the actors — sell Tyler and Holly through someone else's eyes before asking for anything.

8. **ISHMAM** — "Tyler's pomp can be seen from across the room. For Holly, you have to see her in her personality."
9. **SALMAN** — "Holly can hold a silence longer than anyone I've worked with — and make it feel like the loudest thing in the room."

*[Liking / social proof — the cast is vouched for by peers, not self-promoted.]*

---

## BEAT 5 — THE ASK (~1:07–2:20)
This is the persuasion engine. Order matters: humility → sacrifice + deadline → what money buys → specific number + trust → recognition → honest urgency → generous close.

10. **BASIL** — "Our budget's not huge for a movie — but for us, it's everything. Food, permits, lights."

11. **DEVIN** — "Every crew member here is a volunteer. What we're asking for is what volunteers can't cover — insurance, permits, food — until October."
   *[Scarcity — this is the only deadline in the whole video. Do not cut it.]*

12. **SALMAN** — "If you give us a little, you're giving us more time — time to get the shot right, instead of the shot that fits."

13. **ISHMAM** — "If you give, you're supporting the person holding the boom pole in a cold October field."

14. **SHAFI** — "We're asking for three thousand dollars. Every dollar becomes a light, a meal, a permit that keeps us in the park. I promise it's going where we said it would."
   *[Authority + trust — the only line in the video with a hard number and a direct promise. Director-to-camera.]*

15. **HOLLY** — "If you have room to give, I would love your name on the list of people who made this possible."
   *[Reciprocity — backers get something back: credit, belonging.]*

16. **TYLER** — "If it doesn't get funded, it doesn't happen. It's that direct. So if this speaks to you — give what you can."
   *[Loss aversion, stated as plainly as possible, from an actor rather than crew — different register right before the close.]*

17. **HOLLY (final line)** — "And if you cannot give money — share the link. Tell one person about the film. That is also witness. That is also enough."
   *[Closes on inclusion, not a transaction. This is the last thing viewers hear — recency means it's what they'll remember. Also gives non-donors a real action, which lifts overall response.]*

**Pledge/link overlay comes up under this final line and holds through the end card.**

## IF YOU NEED TO HIT A HARD 2:00
Cut in this order — each removal costs the least persuasive value per second:

1. Cut Holly's "name on the list" line (15) — reciprocity is nice-to-have, not load-bearing. Saves ~7s.
2. Tighten the cold-open hold by half a second and trim the title card to 1s. Saves ~1–2s.
3. If still over, cut Samiha (5) and let Sana's line carry Beat 2 alone — Sana's line is the stronger of the two. Saves ~6s.

Do not cut: the "until October" deadline (11), the $3,000 + trust line (14), or the final Holly line (17). Those three are doing the actual persuasive work.
